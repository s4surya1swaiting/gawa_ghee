From Bengal Farms to Global Kitchens

Every spoon of Gawa Ghee carries a 20-year vow: the same cows, the same hands, the same purity. We are not a new brand chasing a trend - we are an old brand telling its story to the world.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver pure, unadulterated ghee to every kitchen — from family homes to five-star hotel pantries — by maintaining end-to-end quality control across our own dairy farms and modern processing facilities in West Bengal. We are committed to zero-compromise purity and on-time institutional logistics.

Chronological Infrastructure Milestones

2006

Foundation

Establishment of specialized dairy sourcing networks within West Bengal, laying the root of our purity pledge.

2010

Logistics Expansion

Launch of expanded processing facilities to support growing regional demand and scale production.

2015

Distributor Network Growth

Onboarding major tier-1 supply networks across eastern territories, establishing distribution dominance.

2020

Institutional Supply Pivot

Securing strict regulatory approvals for military supply lines, including Indian Army Canteens.

2026

Pan-India Enterprise Launch

Nationwide deployment of advanced digital B2B systems for global and rapid local supply pipelines.

Our Vision

To scale Bengal's richest dairy heritage into a globally recognized standard of purity. By blending traditional wood-churned craftsmanship with robust modern logistics, we aim to empower hotels, food manufacturers, and distribution networks worldwide with ghee that is traceably pure, ethically sourced, and unconditionally consistent.
